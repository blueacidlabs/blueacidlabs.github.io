Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

At Blue Acid Lab (accessible at https://blueacidlabs.github.io/), protecting visitor and user privacy is a core priority. This Privacy Policy describes what information is collected when you visit our website or use our mobile utility applications, and how that information is used.

1. Advertising & Google AdSense / AdMob

We use Google AdSense on our website and Google AdMob in our mobile applications to serve advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and device identifiers to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our services or other websites and apps.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve relevant ads to users based on their visit to Blue Acid Lab and other sites across the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.

2. Log Files & Technical Diagnostics

Like most standard websites, our hosting infrastructure (GitHub Pages) automatically collects basic server log data when pages are requested. This information may include IP addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), referring/exit pages, and date/time stamps. These logs are used strictly for security, abuse prevention, and site reliability and are not linked to personally identifiable information.

3. Mobile Utility Applications

Blue Acid Lab develops everyday utility apps such as Calculators, Scanners, and Touch tools. Unless explicitly stated inside a specific application, our utility tools process user inputs locally on your device and do not require account registration or sell personal data to third parties.

4. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Blue Acid Lab's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or external websites. We encourage you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of third-party ad servers, such as the Google Advertising Privacy & Terms, for more detailed information on their practices and opt-out instructions.

5. Children's Information

Blue Acid Lab does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through our services, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove such records.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at blue.acid.labs@gmail.com or visit our official GitHub organization at github.com/blueacidlabs.
